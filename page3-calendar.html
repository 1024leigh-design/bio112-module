The Weekly Rhythm
📖

Part 1: Before You Come to Class

Read the assigned chapter. Review the lecture slides posted on Canvas the week before. Write your lab prelab protocol in your lab notebook and submit the photo to Canvas before 5:29 PM on the day of lab.

📝

Part 2: Submit Prelabs and Post Labs

Prelabs are due before lab starts. Post labs are due the following week -- check the calendar for exact due dates. Do not wait until the night before. These are points for work you already did.

🧠

Part 3: Prepare for Quizzes and Exams

Quizzes happen in the first 15 minutes of lecture. Most class days follow lecture then lab, with a dinner break around 7 PM. Review your notes, do flashcards, and check the weekend update (posted on Canvas as an announcement Thursday or Friday) for what is coming next.

How Difficulty Builds

Microbiology content is cumulative -- each unit assumes you know the last one. Hover each bar to see what it covers. And no, the final is NOT cumulative.

Unit 1Unit 2Unit 3 ⚠️Unit 4 ⚠️⚠️Final (not cumulative)
Foundations of Microbiology (Ch 1-4): history, microscopy, cell structure. Vocab and concepts. Manageable -- but don't get fooled.
Exam 1
Foundations
Microbial Metabolism (Ch 5, 8, 9): metabolism, genomics, recombinant DNA. More abstract. Builds on cell bio from Unit 1.
Exam 2
Metabolism
Biotechnology and Microbe Growth and Death (Ch 6, 7, 9, 10): growth, control of microbes, classification, ID. Gets dense fast. Do not fall behind here.
Exam 3
Biotech + Growth
Bacteria and Viruses (Ch 11, 13, 14): specific bacteria, viruses, disease mechanisms. Huge memorization load. Start early. This is where most students struggle.
Exam 4
Bacteria + Viruses
Microbial Control and the Immune System (Ch 15-17, 20): innate and adaptive immunity, drug therapy, vaccines. NOT cumulative -- covers only this unit.
Final
Control + Immunity
🔥 Units 3 and 4 are the hardest. The memorization load spikes and the material gets very specific. Plan extra study time for these. Multiple students have described Unit 3 as hitting them "like a truck" after the first two felt manageable.

📌 The final exam is NOT cumulative. It covers only Ch 15-17 and 20. I get asked this a lot.
