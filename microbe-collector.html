How to play
  • 🧫 Tap a spot to swab it. You'll grow one random microbe. You can grow the same one many times in a row!
  • 🎯 Swab well! Tap when the swab hits the glowing zone, or tap each spot as it lights up. A perfect swab makes rare microbes 3× as likely.
  • 🔬 Answer its questions to catch it: up to 3 stars (lose one per wrong answer). Catch it again to level it up (max Lv 5) with new questions.
  • 🏆 Score: 10 points per star, a bonus for ◆ uncommon ★ rare 👑 legendary finds, and +25 for every full set.
