TIP #1 -- THE BIG ONE

Come to Office Hours

I'm not exaggerating: my most successful students come to office hours regularly, not just before exams. I cannot help you if I don't know you. Students who show up, ask questions, and work through material with me in person consistently outperform students who go it alone. The room is open, the coffee is not provided, but the help is free.

Mon 2:30 to 5:30 PM
Wed 4:30 to 5:30 PM
BV303
Pronto DM during hours = instant reply
👥

Make a Study Group

Find classmates and meet up outside of class in person. Students who study together consistently do better. When you explain something to someone else, you actually learn it. Don't underestimate this one -- put your pride aside and connect with your classmates.

🗣️

Talk in Class

Ask questions. Yell out answers. You don't need to raise your hand in my class -- just speak up. The faster we move through questions together, the sooner we get to lab. Participation helps you process what you're hearing in real time.

🃏

Make Flashcards

Be specific with your wording -- vague flashcards make vague answers. Review them once a day, even briefly. Daily exposure builds the memory trace more effectively than one big cramming session. Microbiology is dense vocab and this is your best friend for it.

📺

Watch YouTube Videos

I post YouTube videos for every lecture set -- mostly Amoeba Sisters and Bozeman Science (ngl). Watch while commuting, while doing dishes, whenever. 10 minutes makes a real difference. Every new way you see the material helps it stick.

🤖

Use MicrobeAI

Ask it to quiz you, explain concepts different ways, or walk you through a pathway. Great for personalized review and studying on your own schedule.

📚

Read the Book

Even reading the chapter summary and bolded terms before lecture makes a real difference. The textbook is also free on OpenStax -- no excuses. The more ways you are exposed to the material, the better it sticks.

💬

Check Canvas and Pronto Daily

This is where everything lives -- assignments, announcements, due dates, the Daily Study Question. Set a notification. Make it a habit. Missing an announcement is not an excuse for a missed deadline.

📢

Read the Weekend Update

Posted as a Canvas announcement Thursday or Friday. It covers everything for the coming week -- what is due, what to prepare, what to bring. This is your weekly GPS for the course. Do not skip it.

The More Ways You See It, the Better

Microbiology sticks through repetition across different formats. Each new way you engage with the material builds a stronger memory trace. Do all of them. Seriously.

Do Not Lose Easy Points
📓
Turn in your pre/post labs. Last semester, multiple students were within a few points of a higher letter grade. These are points for work you already did in lab. Photograph your notebook and submit before 5:29 PM. Don't let this one slide.
📅
Know your due dates. Check the syllabus, check Canvas, read the weekend update. No late work is accepted -- not even close-to-late.
📝
Take quizzes seriously. Treat every quiz like a mini exam. They add up to 300 points between lecture and lab and revisiting that material early pays off when you hit exam week. Missing even one hurts.
⏰
Show up on time. Quizzes start in the first 15 minutes of class. If you walk in late, you start from where we are -- no extra time. Three absences and you are dropped.
🤖
Do not use AI on assignments. An em dash in your write-up means an automatic zero. A rough draft you wrote yourself will always be better than a zero.
