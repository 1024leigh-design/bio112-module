Lab Safety

Know this before you set foot in BV303

🧥

Attire

Lab coat + closed-toe shoes + long pants. No sandals, shorts, skirts, or watches.

🚨

Spills

Any spill -- chemicals or organisms -- notify instructor immediately. Do not try to handle it on your own first.

🗑️

Disposal

All broken glass and contaminated items go through me. See the Disposal tab for specifics on each material.

🔬

Microscope

Stage all the way down. Lens on 4x or 10x. Arm out. Clean lenses. Cord wrapped. Carried with two hands.

🧼

First + Last Thing Every Lab

Disinfect the benchtop and wash your hands. Every single time.

💧

Water Bottles

In your backpack. Not on the bench. Not by your feet. In your backpack.

Your microscope is your responsibility for the entire semester. These are brand new scopes with internal cameras. Report any issues to me as soon as you notice them.

  1. Lower the stage all the way down before moving anything else.
  2. Rotate the nosepiece to the 4x or 10x objective before storing.
  3. Clean all lenses with lens paper only -- never regular paper or cloth.
  4. Turn off the light.
  5. Wrap the electrical cord neatly around the microscope base.
  6. Carry with two hands: one on the arm, one under the base. Arm facing out.
  7. Store in the cabinet facing toward the back.
You can take photos using the internal camera -- bring a flash drive. Flash drives required to save your images.
Safety enforcement: I will give you one warning if lab safety policies are not followed (including PPE). After that, points will be deducted from your pre/post labs. If you violate safety policies more than twice in a single lab day -- even minor violations -- you will be asked to leave and will receive a zero on the assignment with no opportunity to make it up.
